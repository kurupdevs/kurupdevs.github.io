faq.

Straight answers.

Is KuruBeats really free?

Yes. 100% free, no premium tier, no ads, no in-app purchases. It's open source under the GPL-3.0 license, maintained by kurupdevs.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. Install the APK, open it, play music. No sign-up, no Google login, no phone number.

How do I install it?

Download the APK from the GitHub releases page and install it on your Android phone (Android 8.0+). You may need to allow "install from unknown sources" once.

Can it play my downloaded MP3s?

Yes — it scans your phone for local audio files and plays them with full controls, playlists and stats.

Does YouTube Music streaming work?

Yes, without logging in — with one caveat: on some networks YouTube's bot checks temporarily block anonymous playback (error 2000). This is being worked on; local playback and everything else works everywhere.

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no ads and no trackers. Playlists, stats and settings stay on your device.

Who made KuruBeats?

kurupdevs — a 17-year-old open-source developer. See more of his work or follow him on GitHub.
