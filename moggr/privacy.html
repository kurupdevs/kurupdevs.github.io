Privacy Policy

Moggr · Last updated October 2026

Moggr is a free face-rating app. This policy explains what data the app touches, in plain words.

Your face photos

Face scans are processed entirely on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, by us or anyone else. Temporary scan images are deleted automatically after each scan.

Coach chat

The optional Coach tab is the only feature that uses the internet. When you chat with the coach, only text is sent — your scan measurements (numbers like scores and ratios) and the messages you type. Your photos are never sent, and your real name is never sent (the coach only sees you as "User"). The chat is powered by the Pollinations text API; we don't control their servers, so don't share anything in chat you wouldn't want a stranger to read.

Stored on your device

Your profile and preferences are stored encrypted on your phone. Google Drive backup is disabled for app data, so it never leaves your device through backups either.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-ups.
  • No analytics, no tracking SDKs, no ads.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone — there is no "we" server collecting anything in the first place.

Internet use

The app connects to the internet only for coach chat and to check for app updates. Everything else works offline.

Children

Moggr doesn't ask for your age or identity. If you're under 13, please use the app with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
